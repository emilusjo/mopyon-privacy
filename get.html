Mòpyon

Five in a row, on a notebook page.

Senk nan yon ranje, sou yon paj kaye.

The paper-and-pencil game from the back of the class. Get five of your marks in a row before the computer does: across, down or diagonally. 240 levels, a new sketched place on every page, and your own radio with Compas, Gospel, Hip-Hop and R&B.

Mòpyon se yon jwèt nou jwe nan kaye ak 2 koulè plim, sitou nan klas lekòl. Pou ou genyen, fè 5 nan yon ranje: vètikal, orizontal oubyen dyagonal. Jwe mòpyon pou'w kò'w oubyen envite yon zanmi jwe ave'w epi tande anpil bel mizik pandan jwe.
