Mòpyon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Mòpyon ("the game", "we") is a five-in-a-row paper-and-pencil game by Jocelyn Emilus. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. Mòpyon does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email address or location, and does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To let you carry on where you stopped, the game saves a few things only on your own phone, tablet or computer:

  • your level, wins, losses and streak,
  • the match you are in the middle of,
  • your choices in Settings (volume, pen colours, genre and similar),
  • the names and score you type in for Two Players.

This information never leaves your device. If you delete the game, or choose Settings → Reset my progress, it is erased. We cannot see it and cannot recover it.

Advertising, tracking and analytics

The current version of the game has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it uses none of the advertising or tracking features of your device.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to the game

We may add features later, such as accounts, online play with friends, a paid upgrade, or advertising. If we do, we will update this policy before the feature is released, say plainly what information it uses and why, and ask for your permission where the law or the app store requires it. The date at the top shows when the policy last changed.
