Help with the five-in-a-row paper-and-pencil game

Contact us

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email emilusjo@gmail.com. Please tell us your phone or tablet model and what you saw. We read every message.

Common questions

How do I play?

Open Settings → How to play in the game. In short: get exactly five of your marks in a row before the computer does. Six or more does not count.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play the computer and Two Players as a guest. An account (Sign in with Apple or Google) saves your level online so you can pick it up on another device, and lets you play a friend online. Sign in from Settings → Account.

How do I play a friend online?

Sign in, then choose Play a friend online on the Home screen. Start a game and send the code to your friend, or type the code your friend sent you. If you close the app, open the same screen and tap Continue the game.

The game does not remember my level

If you are a guest, your level and settings are saved only on your own device, and they are lost if you delete the game or reset your phone. If you sign in, your level is saved online too, so signing in on the new device brings it back.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Account → Delete my account. It deletes your account and the progress we keep online. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

I bought Mòpyon Plus on another device

Open Mòpyon Plus and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple account that bought it.

How do I start over?

Open Settings → Your progress → Reset my progress. It goes back to Beginner, level 1.

How do I get more songs?

Songs unlock as you rank up. Locked songs show a padlock and the level that opens them. Mòpyon Plus opens every song.

There is no sound

Check the volume on your device and that the ringer or silent switch is not muting it, then check the Music and Sound effects sliders in Settings.
